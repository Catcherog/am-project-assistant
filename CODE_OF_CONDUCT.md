# Code of Conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Discuss evidence and technical decisions without personal attacks. Welcome questions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or confidential business records to demonstrate a point.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or close discussions that violate these standards. When practical, they will explain the reason and provide a path to a constructive discussion.

For reports involving private information, use the repository's private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For an ordinary moderation concern, contact the maintainer through a GitHub issue without including private details or identifying affected people unnecessarily.
